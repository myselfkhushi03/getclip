How it works

Three steps, no account and nothing to install.

Formats and quality

The list you see is built from what the source actually publishes, so you never get an option that does not exist.

YouTube

  • Video: 144p up to 2160p (4K), when the video is available in that resolution
  • MP3: 64, 128 or 320 kbps
  • M4A: AAC audio

Instagram

  • Video: HD and SD, when both are offered
  • Audio: M4A or MP3, extracted from the video

Video streams are downloaded as published and are not compressed again. YouTube serves 1080p and above as separate video and audio streams, which are joined into one file without re-encoding, so those downloads take a little longer. MP3 files are encoded from the source audio, so they can match the original but never exceed it.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a resolution missing for my video?

Only resolutions that the uploader and the platform publish are listed. Many videos are not available in 1440p or 4K.

Is the quality reduced?

Video is not compressed again. For audio, M4A keeps the original AAC stream when the source provides one. MP3 is created by encoding the source audio, so a 320 kbps MP3 is not better than the original track.

Can I download private videos or stories?

No. Private and members-only videos cannot be downloaded. Instagram stories work only when they are public and reachable, and some will fail.

Why did my download fail?

The video may be private, removed, age restricted or still live. A busy server can also refuse a request for a moment. Check the message shown under the link field, then try again.

Do you keep my files?

No. A file is prepared when you ask for it, stays available for a few minutes, and is then deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is it legal to download videos?

That depends on the content and where you live. Downloading material you do not own or have permission to use can break copyright law and the platform's terms. Use this tool for your own videos, public domain or Creative Commons content, or with the owner's permission.
