Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy explains what Getclip processes when you use the downloader. We aim to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts on this site.

What we process

  • Links you submit. The YouTube or Instagram link you paste is sent to our server so it can look up the video and prepare the file you request.
  • Prepared files. Files are created on our server only when you ask for them. They stay available for a few minutes so your browser can download them, and are then deleted automatically.
  • Technical data. Your IP address and basic request information are processed to deliver the site, limit abuse and keep the service stable. Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

What we do not do

  • We do not require you to create an account or give us your name or email address.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics on this site.

Cookies and local storage

The site does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser to track you.

Third parties

To fetch a video, our server contacts YouTube or Instagram, and those services receive the request together with our server's IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to that traffic. Video thumbnails are fetched through our server so your browser does not contact those services directly.

Your choices

Because we do not keep accounts or profiles, there is no personal profile to access or delete. If you want a prepared file removed before it expires, tell us and we will remove it. If you believe we hold personal data about you, you may ask us to confirm or delete it.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If we change how data is handled, for example by adding analytics or advertising, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes place.
